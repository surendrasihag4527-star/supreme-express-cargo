Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

At Supreme Express Cargo, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal and business information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, company name, email address, phone number, and physical address when you request a quote, use our services, or contact us for support. We also collect tracking details for shipments to ensure accurate delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our logistics and transport services.
  • Process transactions and send you related information, including invoices and confirmations.
  • Communicate with you regarding your shipments, quotes, and customer service inquiries.
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve any disputes.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted operational partners strictly for the purpose of fulfilling our logistics services (e.g., third-party carriers involved in PTL or 3PL operations). We implement standard security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.
