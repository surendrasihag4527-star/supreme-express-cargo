Your Trusted Logistics Partner in Silvassa & Daman

The Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (especially Silvassa) represents one of Western India's most highly dense manufacturing zones. With hundreds of plastic extrusion units, pharmaceutical plants, paper packaging mills, and consumer goods factories operating daily, maintaining uninterrupted supply lines is key. Supreme Express Cargo has emerged as the best transporter in Silvassa and surrounding industrial hubs.

As a premier logistics company in Vapi Daman Silvassa, our close proximity to the Vapi border allows us to orchestrate quick dispatches. We specialize in daily factory pickups, FTL container lines, and PTL cargo consolidation to connect Silvassa and Daman directly with national trade hubs in Delhi-NCR, Maharashtra, and Southern India.

Connecting Silvassa & Daman with Delhi NCR & Gujarat

Our transport network links Silvassa directly with the rest of Gujarat and northern markets. We run daily express dispatches along the Delhi to Vapi/Daman/Silvassa transport route. Whether you require a high-capacity Full truck load service India or cost-efficient Part load transport service, our team manages all logistics details including e-Way bills and local checkpost documentation.
