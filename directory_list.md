## Optimization Guide for Directory Submissions

To get the most ranking power from these directories:

1. **NAP Consistency**: Ensure your Business Name, Address, and Phone Number are *exactly* the same on all platforms. If you write "Supreme Express Cargo" on one site, do not write "Supreme Express Cargo Pvt. Ltd." on another.
2. **Keyword-Rich Descriptions**: Instead of just "We are a transport company," use:
   > *"Supreme Express Cargo is a top-rated transport company providing Full Truck Load (FTL) logistics, Part Truck Load (PTL) cargo shipping, and warehousing services in Vapi, Daman, Silvassa, and Delhi NCR. We cater to manufacturing units and corporate shippers with a fleet of containerized trucks, offering safe and reliable point-to-point freight movement."*
3. **Deep Linking**: Don't link everything to your homepage.
   - If a directory allows you to specify service landing pages, link the Silvassa citation directly to `https://www.supremeexpresscargo.in/best-transporter-silvassa.html` and Vapi to `https://www.supremeexpresscargo.in/best-transporter-vapi.html`.
4. **Acquire Reviews**: For local directories (like Google Business Profile and Justdial), ask your regular corporate clients or truck owners to leave positive reviews mentioning keywords (e.g., *"Best transporter in Vapi for timely FTL deliveries"*). This dramatically boosts search engine visibility.
